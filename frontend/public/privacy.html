Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Core principle: SGEN does not store personal data. No names, email addresses, or other personally identifying information is ever saved in our database.

1. What we do

SGEN is a training statistics application. When you connect your Garmin Connect account, we receive activity and wellness data (training sessions, heart rate, sleep, etc.) via Garmin's Push API so we can display statistics and perform calculations in the app.

2. What we store

  • Training metrics: activity times, duration, distance, power, heart rate, speed, cadence
  • Wellness data: sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, body composition metrics
  • A pseudonymous Garmin user identifier (a random string, not your name or email)
  • Encrypted OAuth access tokens, required to maintain your connection

3. What we do NOT store

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any personal profile information
  • Any data beyond what is technically required to provide the service

4. How data is used and shared

Your data is used solely to display statistics and calculations inside the app to you. Your Garmin data is never shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. It stays on this server.

5. Deleting your data

You can disconnect your Garmin account at any time from the Profile page. Doing so revokes the connection and permanently deletes all your locally stored Garmin data. You may also revoke access directly in Garmin Connect, which notifies us to delete your data automatically.

6. Data security

Access tokens are stored AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest. All communication is over HTTPS.

SGEN — self-hosted training statistics. This service stores no personal data.
